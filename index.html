How it works

ROM bytes for this sound

Sound effects are little programs inside the game. You can retune them (pitch, tone, slides, timing, the notes in their tables) but each keeps its basic shape.

Songs

All songs share one small space in the cartridge, about 1.5 KB. To make a song longer, make another one shorter, turn off a song's harmony, or silence a song you don't need.
